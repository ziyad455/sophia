# Sophia Product Brief

Sophia is a philosophy reading companion.

It helps users:
- read philosophy comfortably
- understand difficult ideas
- reflect on what they read
- save meaningful insights
- build long-term philosophical understanding

Sophia is focused only on philosophy for the MVP.

Target authors:
- Schopenhauer
- Nietzsche
- Dostoevsky
- Camus
- Plato
- Aristotle
- Marcus Aurelius
- Seneca

Sophia competes on experience, not raw AI intelligence.

The core belief:

Reading philosophy is not about finishing books.
It is about understanding ideas, questioning assumptions, and gradually building your own philosophy.
